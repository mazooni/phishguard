PhishGuard

An on-device phishing watchdog for iPhone, with a guard number that warns you during a scam call.

This is the real iOS app, streamed from a cloud iPhone simulator, running in demo mode.

Tap the phone to start a session. It opens on a fictional week of flagged emails and calls, every one of them scored by the app's own detector. Each session starts fresh and ends after a few minutes of inactivity.

What to try

  • Alerts: scroll the flagged emails, grouped by day. Red is high risk, orange medium, yellow low.
  • Open an alert: the confidence, the summary, and Why it was flagged with the evidence behind every reason.
  • Calls: past scam calls that Call Guard caught, with what the caller said that gave it away.
  • Settings › Demo: Simulate a phishing email or Simulate a scam call, then watch the notification arrive. Allow notifications when the phone asks.
  • Settings › Diagnostics: run a test scan over the 33 bundled sample emails and see how each one scores.

What this demo does not do

Live calls and mailbox sign-in are switched off here: this page has no phone line and no mail account behind it. The Calls tab shows past detections only, and adding a Gmail, Outlook or IMAP account is not available. On a real iPhone, calls to your guard number are transcribed and scored while you talk, and new email is checked as it arrives.

How it works

Every email is checked on the phone itself by a rule engine and a local language model; the email is thrown away and only the verdict is kept. Call Guard gives you a guard phone number: calls to it ring your phone as usual while a small relay transcribes and scores the conversation, and warns you, with an urgent notification and a spoken warning only you hear, while the call is still going.
